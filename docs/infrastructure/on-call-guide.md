# Kirmya SRE On-Call & Escalation Operations Guide

## 1. On-Call Rotations & Incident Escalation
- **Primary / Secondary Rotations**: 24/7 weekly rotational coverage across core platform engineering and SRE teams.
- **Automated Paging Policies**: P0/P1 incidents trigger automated multi-channel alerts (SMS, PagerDuty, Slack, Push).
- **Incident Commander Role**: Designated commander manages war rooms, coordinates mitigation, and posts real-time public status updates.
