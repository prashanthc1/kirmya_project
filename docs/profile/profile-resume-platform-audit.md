# Kirmya Career Profile, Resume & Professional Portfolio Comprehensive Audit

## Executive Summary
This document audits the Candidate Professional Profile suite, Multi-Version Resume Engine, PDF Export Pipeline, Open-to-Work Controls, AI Resume Optimization Safeguards, and Recruiter Search Discovery across Kirmya.

---

## 1. Career Identity Ecosystem Overview
- **Authoritative Persistence**: PostgreSQL is the transactional source of truth for user profiles, work experience records, education histories, skill proficiencies, and resume versions.
- **Strict Privacy & Field Shielding**: Private contact details, compensation expectations, and career target preferences remain shielded from public indexes and recruiter searches unless explicitly authorized.
- **AI Career Assistance Boundaries**: AI suggestions are purely advisory and strictly bound against fabricating credentials, hallucinating employment histories, or altering candidate truth.
- **100% Free Career Advancement**: Resume generation, PDF compilation, unlimited resume variants, and portfolio hosting are 100% free with zero paywalls.
