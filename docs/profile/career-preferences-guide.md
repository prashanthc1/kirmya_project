# Kirmya Career Preferences & Open-to-Work Configuration Guide

## 1. Defining Career Preferences & Visibility
- **Target Role Criteria**: Specify desired job titles, industries, employment types (Full-Time, Contract, Internship), and remote/hybrid preferences.
- **Compensation & Location**: Record salary expectations and geographic preferences; kept private from public views and disclosed only to matching recruiters where authorized.
- **Open-to-Work Toggles**: Signal job seeking status selectively to recruiters or the broader professional network.
