# Kirmya People Search & Professional Discovery Manual

## 1. Professional Profile Discovery & Privacy Safeguards
- **Visibility-Grounded Indexing**: Only users who have enabled public or member profile discoverability appear in search results.
- **Exclusion of Blocked Users**: Bilateral exclusion filters ensure blocked or restricted accounts never appear in discovery feeds.
- **Protected Attribute Exclusion**: Ranking models completely ignore age, gender, race, religion, and other sensitive personal attributes.
