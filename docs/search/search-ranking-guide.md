# Kirmya Search Relevance & Ranking Optimization Manual

## 1. Relevance Scoring & Ranking Weights
- **BM25 Text Relevance**: Core keyword matching weighted across title, verified skills, job description, and organization name.
- **Freshness & Decay Decay**: Time-decay scoring prioritizes newly posted vacancies and active professional profiles.
- **Trust & Safety Demotion**: Accounts or job postings under active moderation review receive negative ranking boosts or complete exclusion.
