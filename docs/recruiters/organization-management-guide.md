# Kirmya Organization Management & Teammate Administration Guide

## 1. Multi-User Organization Administration
- **Inviting Teammates**: Generate single-use, cryptographically secure invitation tokens tied to specific organization scopes.
- **Company Verification**: Submit employer corporate credentials for review by Platform Trust & Safety administrators.
- **Hiring Team Scoping**: Assign recruiters and interviewers to specific job openings to limit candidate data exposure.
