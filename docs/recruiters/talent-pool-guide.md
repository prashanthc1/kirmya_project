# Kirmya Talent Pools & Saved Candidate Management Manual

## 1. Talent Pool Structuring
- **Private Organization Pools**: Candidates saved to talent pools are strictly scoped to the recruiter's organization and invisible to external employers.
- **Categorization Tags**: Organize prospective talent by functional domain, seniority level, or target opening timeframe.
- **Candidate Consent & Privacy**: Candidates can revoke recruiter visibility at any time, which immediately flags their bookmark as private.
