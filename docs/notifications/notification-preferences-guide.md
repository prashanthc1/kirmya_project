# Kirmya Communication Preferences, Quiet Hours & Delivery Channels

## 1. Notification Preferences Matrix
- **Category Granularity**: Users can independently toggle In-App, Email, and Push notifications per category (`Jobs`, `Applications`, `Interviews`, `Networking`, `Messages`, `Communities`).
- **Quiet Hours (DND)**: Schedule quiet windows (e.g. 10 PM to 7 AM local time) during which non-critical push and email notifications are held.
- **Security Override**: Critical security alerts (e.g. password changes, new logins) unconditionally bypass quiet hours.
