# Kirmya Notification Privacy & Data Shielding

## 1. Zero Private Content Exposure
- **Private Messages**: Notifications indicate sender name and conversation context without embedding raw message body text in email payloads.
- **ATS Recruiter Notes**: Internal notes, scorecards, and hiring ratings are strictly excluded from all notification payloads.

---

## 2. Organization Boundary Isolation
Recruiter notifications (e.g. `application.submitted`) are dispatched exclusively to verified recruiters authorized for the specific organization and job.
