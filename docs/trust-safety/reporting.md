# Kirmya User Reporting Pipelines & Evidence Handling

## 1. Supported Report Targets
- **User Profiles**: Impersonation, offensive avatar/bio, spam behavior.
- **Job Postings**: Fake job listings, fee demands, phishing links.
- **Direct Messages**: Harassment, abuse, unsolicited commercial solicitation.
- **Community Posts & Comments**: Misinformation, hostile behavior, policy violations.

---

## 2. Privacy & Evidence Isolation
- Reporters attach optional message context or screenshots.
- Only assigned moderators with explicit Trust & Safety permissions can access attached evidence.
