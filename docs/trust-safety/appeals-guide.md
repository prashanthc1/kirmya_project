# Kirmya Account Enforcement Appeals Guide & Resolution Protocol

## 1. Submitting and Reviewing Appeals
- **Eligibility**: Any user facing feature restrictions, temporary suspension, or account ban may file an appeal within 14 calendar days of enforcement.
- **Submission Process**: Users access the Appeals Portal via account notification or restricted login screen.
- **Review Standard**: Assigned to a Senior Moderator not involved in the original decision; decisions rendered within 48 business hours.
