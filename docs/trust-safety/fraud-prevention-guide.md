# Kirmya Job Fraud, Phishing & Recruiter Impersonation Defense Guide

## 1. Common Fraud Vectors in Career Recovery
- **Advance-Fee Schemes**: Fraudulent recruiters demanding payment for equipment or background checks.
- **Phishing Portals**: Fake interview links harvesting candidate credentials or government IDs.
- **Detection Rules**: Keyword tripwires on job posts (`payment required`, `crypto`, `telegram interview`, `wire transfer`) automatically quarantine posts for human moderation.
