# Kirmya Fraud Detection & Job Scam Protection

## 1. Fake Job & Recruiter Impersonation Detection
- **Domain Verification**: Validates recruiter email domain against claimed company corporate domain.
- **Trigger Keyword Scanning**: Scans job descriptions for prohibited fee requests (e.g. "pay for background check", "wire transfer for equipment").
- **Unverified Recruiter Badging**: Jobs published by unverified domains display a caution badge alerting applicants to exercise vigilance.
