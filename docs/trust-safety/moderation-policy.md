# Kirmya Platform Moderation Policy & Community Standards

## 1. Prohibited Activities
- **Fraudulent Recruitment & Fee Requests**: Requesting candidate payment for job interviews or training.
- **Harassment & Hate Speech**: Abusive direct messages, discriminatory job postings, or community bullying.
- **Spam & Mass Automated Outreach**: Scraping user profiles or sending unsolicited marketing broadcasts.

---

## 2. Enforcement Progression Ladder
1. **Official Warning**: Educational notification regarding community standard breach.
2. **Feature Restriction**: 7-day restriction on messaging or job posting.
3. **Temporary Suspension**: 14 to 30 day account lockout.
4. **Permanent Ban**: Full account termination and domain block for severe violations.
