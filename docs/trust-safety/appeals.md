# Kirmya Moderation Appeals Workflow

## 1. User Appeals Desk (`/appeals`)
- Users subject to warnings, restrictions, or suspensions can submit a formal appeal detailing their counter-arguments or remediation steps.

---

## 2. Independent Review Protocol
- Appeals are automatically routed to a different moderator from the one who issued the initial enforcement action, ensuring impartial review and eliminating bias.
- Potential outcomes: `Upheld`, `Reduced`, `Reversed`.
