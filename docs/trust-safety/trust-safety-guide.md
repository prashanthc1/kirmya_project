# Kirmya Community Safety & Trust Guidelines for Members

## 1. Professional Safety Commitments
- **Zero Toleration for Exploitation**: Kirmya is 100% free for job seekers; any attempt to charge for referrals, interviews, or application processing results in immediate account suspension.
- **Reporting Mechanism**: Click the reporting flag on any suspicious job, message, profile, or post to instantly route it to the 24/7 Moderation Desk.
- **Privacy & Retaliation Shielding**: Reporter identity is strictly confidential and never revealed to reported entities.
