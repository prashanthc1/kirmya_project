# Kirmya Community Member User Guide

## 1. Engaging in Professional Communities
- **Discovery & Joining**: Search groups by domain (`Tech`, `Career Transition`, `Job Search Strategy`, `Design`) and request membership.
- **Posting & Discussions**: Share career recovery advice, ask for resume feedback, and participate in threaded discussions.
- **Rules & Etiquette**: Respect community rules displayed in the banner modal; off-topic promotion or harassment results in warning or ban.
