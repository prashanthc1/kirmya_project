# Kirmya Community Moderation Desk & Content Safety Manual

## 1. Moderator Queue & Triage Workflow
- **Report Ingestion**: Member reports categorizing spam, harassment, hate speech, scam/fraud, or misleading info.
- **Triage Actions**:
  - **Remove Content**: Deletes offending post/comment and logs reason in audit log.
  - **Warn Member**: Dispatches in-app warning notification with rule citation.
  - **Lock Thread**: Prevents new comments while preserving discussion readability.
  - **Ban Member**: Restricts user from group and auto-rejects future join requests.
