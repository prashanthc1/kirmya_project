# Kirmya Community Admin Operations & Governance Guide

## 1. Group Governance & Role Hierarchy
- **Owner**: Creator of the group; holds sole authority to transfer ownership or delete the community.
- **Admin**: Configures group metadata, rules, visibility (Public/Private), and assigns Moderator roles.
- **Moderator**: Reviews flagged reports, bans abusive members, pins announcements, and locks threads.
- **Member**: Reads discussions, creates posts, comments, and RSVPs to community events.
