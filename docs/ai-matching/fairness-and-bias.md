# Kirmya AI Matching Fairness & Bias Mitigation

## 1. Bias Prevention Standards
- **Feature Neutrality**: Ranking algorithms utilize exclusively verifiable professional attributes (skills, work history length, certifications, education disciplines).
- **Prohibited Attributes**: Demographic traits (gender, race, age, nationality, zip codes prone to redlining) are excluded from scoring tensors.
- **Regular Disparate Impact Audits**: Recommendation distributions are audited quarterly across cohorts to ensure equitable opportunity exposure.
