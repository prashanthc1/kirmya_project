# Kirmya Recruiter Candidate Screening Assistance

## 1. Recruiter Candidate Discovery (`/recruiter/jobs/:jobId/matches`)
- Surfaces candidates with high skill overlap for published active jobs.
- Respects candidate discovery privacy settings; candidates who opt out of recruiter discovery are never indexed or recommended.

---

## 2. Human-in-the-Loop Governance
- AI screening outputs provide semantic highlights and skill matrices to accelerate resume review.
- Automated auto-rejection is strictly disabled; human recruiters make all progression and hiring decisions.
