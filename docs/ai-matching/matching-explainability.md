# Kirmya Explainable AI Matching Framework

## 1. Structured Match Explanations
Every recommendation returns human-interpretable rationale cards:
- **Skill Alignment**: *"7 of 9 required skills match your profile: Go, PostgreSQL, Docker, Redis..."*
- **Role Seniority**: *"Your 5+ years of software engineering experience aligns with this Senior role."*
- **Work Mode**: *"Matches your preference for 100% remote positions."*

---

## 2. Missing & Transferable Skills
The UI clearly distinguishes between:
- **Required Missing Skills**: Critical requirements needing upskilling.
- **Preferred Missing Skills**: Value-add criteria that do not block application viability.
- **Transferable Skills**: Related competencies (e.g. AWS knowledge transferring to GCP environments).
