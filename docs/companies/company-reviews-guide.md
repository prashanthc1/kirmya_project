# Kirmya Employer Reviews, Moderation & Reputation Manual

## 1. Structured Reviews & Moderation Standards
- **Evaluation Dimensions**: Categorized feedback covering work environment, leadership, career progression, and work-life balance.
- **Trust & Safety Screening**: Submissions pass automated toxicity and profanity tripwires prior to human moderator evaluation.
- **Employer Response Right**: Verified employers can submit professional public responses without altering original reviewer feedback.
