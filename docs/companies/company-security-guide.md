# Kirmya Company Security, Tenant Isolation & Media Safety Manual

## 1. Security Safeguards & Threat Defenses
- **Ownership Verification**: All company update endpoints evaluate organization administrator permissions in session context.
- **Media Safety & Malware Scanning**: Uploaded logos and banner assets are scanned for binary MIME validity and malicious payloads.
- **Review Fraud Prevention**: IP-based rate limiting and behavioral heuristics prevent coordinated review manipulation or harassment campaigns.
