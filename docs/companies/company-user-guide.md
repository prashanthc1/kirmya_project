# Kirmya Company Discovery & Employer Research User Guide

## 1. Candidate Research & Company Following
- **Company Pages (`/companies/[id]`)**: Explore company mission statements, core values, work culture, benefit summaries, and active job openings.
- **Following Companies**: Follow organizations to receive automated alerts whenever new matching vacancies are published.
- **Verified Badges**: Easily identify verified employers backed by platform authentication checks.
