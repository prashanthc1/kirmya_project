# Kirmya Employer Verification & Trust Badge Manual

## 1. Verification Lifecycle & Badge Assurance
- **Verification Workflow**: Organizations submit legal registration documentation and official domain email confirmation.
- **Admin Review Queue**: Platform Trust & Safety administrators evaluate legitimacy; badges are granted strictly through backend authorization.
- **Anti-Fraud Safeguards**: Suspicious or revoked employers immediately lose verified status and active job postings.
