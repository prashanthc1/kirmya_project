# Kirmya Employer Branding, Culture & Benefits Guide

## 1. Structuring Compelling Employer Branding
- **Culture & Values**: Showcase core corporate values, workplace philosophy, and team collaboration styles.
- **Structured Benefits**: Highlight categorized offerings (Health & Wellness, Remote Flexibility, Learning & Tuition, Parental Leave).
- **Sanitized Rich Media**: Media uploads and rich text descriptions are sanitized against script injection and malicious payloads.
