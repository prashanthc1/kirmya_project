# Kirmya Administrative Moderation Queue & Case Triage Manual

## 1. Case Assignment & Queue Management
- **Prioritized Queues**: Flags categorized into Critical, High, Medium, and Low severity with defined review time SLAs.
- **Moderator Assignment**: Cases can be claimed by individual moderators, with automatic release upon 30 minutes of inactivity.
- **Action Verification**: Content takedowns require selecting standardized policy violation categories and entering internal notes.
