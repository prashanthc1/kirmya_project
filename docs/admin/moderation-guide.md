# Kirmya Content Moderation & Queue Management Manual

## 1. Content Triage & Enforcement Actions
- **Prioritized Queues**: Classifies incoming flagged content (Critical, High, Medium, Low) based on severity and potential harm.
- **Action Suite**: Support actions including Approve, Remove, Restrict, and Escalate with mandatory categorization reasons.
- **Content Restoration**: Mistakenly removed posts, comments, or job postings can be safely restored by authorized moderators with audit entries.
