# Kirmya Privacy Operations & Data Subject Request Administration Manual

## 1. DSR Processing & Privacy Safeguards
- **Data Export Generation**: Assembles complete, standardized JSON/ZIP archives of user-submitted profile, resume, and application data.
- **Account Deletion Pipeline**: Orchestrates cascading anonymization and record purging adhering to GDPR/CCPA legal retention constraints.
- **Legal Hold Shielding**: Blocks deletion of records subject to active regulatory or litigation holds with documented admin justifications.
