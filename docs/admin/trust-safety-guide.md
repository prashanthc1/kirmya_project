# Kirmya Trust & Safety Operations & Abuse Prevention Manual

## 1. Fraud & Scam Defense Framework
- **Scam Job Detection**: Automated filters scan job postings for fee-payment requests, deceptive compensation ranges, and suspicious external redirects.
- **Recruiter Verification**: Business domain email verification and document validation prevent corporate impersonation.
- **Fairness Guarantee**: Demographic attributes and protected characteristics are strictly excluded from risk scoring algorithms.
