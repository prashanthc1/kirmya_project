# Kirmya Moderation Appeals & Due Process Operations Manual

## 1. Due Process & Appeals Lifecycle
- **Appeal Eligibility**: Users subjected to account restrictions or content removal can submit structured appeals with supporting explanations.
- **Independent Re-Evaluation**: Appeals are routed to senior Trust & Safety personnel independent of the initial enforcer.
- **Audit Trails**: Every appeal acceptance, rejection, or request for additional information is recorded with explicit justifications.
