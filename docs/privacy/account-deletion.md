# Kirmya Account Deletion & Right to Erasure Workflow

## 1. 30-Day Graceful Deletion Lifecycle
1. **Initiation**: User verifies password and confirms deletion at `/settings/privacy/delete-account`.
2. **Deactivation**: Account is instantly hidden from search, networking, messaging, and recruiter matching.
3. **Grace Period**: User has 30 days to cancel deletion by logging in.
4. **Hard Deletion & Anonymization**: After 30 days, background workers purge user PII and anonymize audit logs.
