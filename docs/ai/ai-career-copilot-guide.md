# Kirmya AI Career Copilot & Interactive Assistant User Guide

## 1. Conversational Career Guidance Features
- **Interactive Career Coaching**: Engage in multi-turn dialogues to explore transition paths, upskilling strategies, and industry trends.
- **Actionable Roadmap Cards**: Receive structured milestones linking directly to verified courses, practice projects, and job openings.
- **User Agency**: AI recommendations require explicit confirmation before applying changes to candidate profiles or resumes.
