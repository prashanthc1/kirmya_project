# Kirmya AI Job Matching & Explainable Recommendations Manual

## 1. Explainable Job Matching Architecture
- **Multi-Vector Semantic Signals**: Matches candidates against job postings based on verified skills, seniority tier, and work preferences.
- **Explainability Badges**: Highlights concrete match rationales ("Matched 8/10 required skills: Go, PostgreSQL, Docker").
- **Zero-Discrimination Enforcement**: Rejection of any demographic or protected attribute in scoring models.
