# Kirmya AI Privacy, Context Minimization & Data Protection Manual

## 1. Zero-Leakage Privacy Architecture
- **Context Minimization**: Sends only the minimal fields necessary for inference; removes email addresses, phone numbers, and exact street addresses.
- **Provider Confidentiality**: Third-party LLM providers are configured with zero-data-retention agreements for model training.
- **Employer Isolation**: Recruiters cannot access candidate AI conversation history, private mock interviews, or memory logs.
