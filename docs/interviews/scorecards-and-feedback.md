# Kirmya Interview Scorecards & Evaluation Framework

## 1. Structured Evaluation Criteria

| Category | Description | Scale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Technical Competency** | Role-specific coding, system design, or domain expertise | 1 to 5 (1=Strong No, 5=Strong Yes) |
| **Problem Solving** | Analytical reasoning, structured breakdown of ambiguity | 1 to 5 |
| **Communication** | Clarity of thought, listening, collaborative discussion | 1 to 5 |
| **Culture & Values** | Alignment with team norms, growth mindset, integrity | 1 to 5 |
| **Overall Recommendation** | Final hiring verdict | `Strong Hire`, `Hire`, `No Hire`, `Strong No Hire` |

---

## 2. Feedback Shielding
Scorecards and individual interviewer feedback are strictly isolated to the recruiter and hiring team. Candidates never receive raw ratings or reviewer identities.
