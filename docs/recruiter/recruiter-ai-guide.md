# Kirmya AI-Assisted Recruiter Copilot & Sourcing Guidance Manual

## 1. Ethical AI Recruiter Assistance Boundaries
- **Assistive Sourcing**: AI tools summarize applicant qualifications, draft inclusive job descriptions, and suggest structured interview questions.
- **Strict Anti-Automated Decision Rule**: The AI layer is strictly forbidden from executing automated rejection, shortlisting, or hiring decisions.
- **Fairness & Non-Discrimination**: Demographic attributes and protected characteristics are stripped before candidate evaluation summaries are generated.
