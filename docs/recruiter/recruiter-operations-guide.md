# SRE Operations Guide: Recruiter Platforms & Organization Triage

## 1. Monitoring & Operational Playbooks
1. **Permission Denied Alerts**: Track spike in 403 Forbidden responses to catch authorization regressions.
2. **Organization Verification Queue**: SRE and Trust & Safety staff review pending employer domain registrations.
3. **Invitation Rate Limits**: Automatic throttling prevents bot-driven mass employee invitation campaigns.
