# Kirmya Hiring Team Collaboration & Role Scoping Guide

## 1. Hiring Team Management & Job Assignment
- **Job-Specific Teams**: Assign specific recruiters, hiring managers, and interviewers to particular job requisitions.
- **Access Partitioning**: Hiring team members only access applicant data and interview schedules for jobs to which they are explicitly assigned.
- **Invitation Lifecycle**: Single-use, cryptographically generated invite tokens ensure authorized onboarding of company teammates.
