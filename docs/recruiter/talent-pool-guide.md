# Kirmya Talent Pools & Candidate Bookmarking Manual

## 1. Talent Pool Organization & Tagging
- **Organization-Private Pools**: Saved candidate collections and custom domain tags remain strictly internal to the recruiter's organization.
- **Candidate Consent Tracking**: If a candidate revokes recruiter visibility, their bookmark status automatically reflects their updated privacy preference.
- **Team Collaboration**: Shared talent pools allow hiring team members to review prospective candidates for future openings.
