# Kirmya Recruiter Privacy, Candidate Shielding & Consent Manual

## 1. Candidate Privacy Enforcement & Data Boundaries
- **Consent-Driven Visibility**: Recruiters access only the specific profile sections and resume variants explicitly authorized by the job seeker.
- **Shielded Candidate Activity**: Candidate private search behavior, bookmark history, and external job applications remain strictly confidential.
- **Evaluation Secrecy**: Candidate notes, scorecards, and internal ratings are sealed from candidates and cross-organization access.
