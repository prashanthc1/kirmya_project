# Kirmya Job Discovery & Personalized Job Feed User Guide

## 1. Job Search & Opportunity Discovery
- **Global Job Search (`/jobs`)**: Search across job titles, verified skills, companies, and locations with instant debounce autocomplete.
- **Advanced Multi-Dimensional Filters**: Filter by work mode (Remote, Hybrid, On-site), experience tier, salary expectations, and posting date.
- **Saved Searches & Instant Alerts**: Save complex search queries and receive immediate, daily, or weekly notifications for new matching vacancies.
