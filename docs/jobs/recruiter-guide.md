# Kirmya Recruiter Hiring Operations & Multi-Seat Collaboration

## 1. Recruiter Permissions & Organization Scoping
- **Multi-Seat Collaboration**: Organization admins invite and manage recruiters with granular job requisition permissions.
- **Candidate Assignment**: Assign specific hiring managers or recruiters to job requisitions.
- **Bulk Stage Actions**: Batch move candidates or send bulk notifications safely with server-side rate limits.
