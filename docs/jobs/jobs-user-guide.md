# Kirmya Job Seeker & Career Recovery User Guide

## 1. Navigating Opportunities & Applications
- **Free Access Guarantee**: Every job search, application, and recruiter connection is 100% free with zero fees or premium tier barriers.
- **Application Tracking**: Monitor live progress of applications in the Candidate Hub (`/applications`).
- **Saved Jobs & Instant Alerts**: Bookmark roles and subscribe to real-time notification alerts matching your target skills and location.
