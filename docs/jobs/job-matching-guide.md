# Kirmya Explainable Job Matching & Recommendation Engine

## 1. Algorithmic Recommendations & Explainability
- **Core Signal Inputs**: Matched on verified candidate skills, career seniority, preferred locations, and industry targets.
- **Explainable Match Badges**: Clear rationales (e.g. `92% Skill Match`, `Remote Eligible`, `Industry Match`) presented to candidates.
- **Fairness & Bias Shield**: Protected demographic attributes are strictly omitted from matching vectors and ranking algorithms.
