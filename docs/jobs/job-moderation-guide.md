# Kirmya Job Moderation & Fraud Prevention Manual

## 1. Automated Fraud Signals & Risk Scoring
- **Rule-Based Triggers**: Automated flagging for keywords like `crypto payment`, `upfront fee`, `telegram only`, `wire transfer`.
- **User Reports Queue**: Candidate report ingestion with severity weighting and admin review queue.
- **Enforcement Actions**: Instant unpublishing, recruiter account suspension, and domain blocklisting.
