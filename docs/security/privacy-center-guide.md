# Kirmya Privacy Center & User Consent Administration Guide

## 1. User Privacy Controls & Consent Management
- **Centralized Privacy Hub (`/settings/privacy`)**: Configure profile discoverability, search engine indexing, and direct messaging permissions.
- **Granular Cookie Consent**: Opt-in/opt-out toggles for non-essential analytical cookies with persistent consent version tracking.
- **Data Protection Rights**: Self-service triggers for downloading account archives or initiating GDPR/CCPA erasure requests.
