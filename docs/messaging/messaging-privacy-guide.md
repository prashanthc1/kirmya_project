# Kirmya Messaging Privacy, Blocking & Data Retention Manual

## 1. Privacy Safeguards & Mutual Blocking
- **Strict Bidirectional Shielding**: Blocking a user immediately prevents subsequent messages and removes existing 1-to-1 conversation previews from their inbox.
- **Read Receipt & Presence Toggles**: Users can independently disable read receipts and online presence indicators under Privacy Settings.
- **Account Deletion & Data Retention**: Upon account deletion, messages are stripped of personal identifiers while preserving dialogue continuity for the recipient.
