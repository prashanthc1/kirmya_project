# Kirmya Crisis Incident Communications & Stakeholder Protocols

## 1. Incident Communication Channels
- **Internal Engineering Bridge**: Dedicated incident audio/chat room for Incident Commander, Tech Lead, and duty engineers.
- **User-Facing Status Page**: Real-time status updates at `https://status.kirmya.com` with pre-approved template advisories.
- **Enterprise / Recruiter Alerts**: Direct email broadcasts for major hiring desk disruptions.
