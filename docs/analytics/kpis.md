# Kirmya Platform KPI & Business Metric Definitions

## 1. Engagement & Activation Metrics
- **DAU / WAU / MAU**: Distinct active users with at least 1 authenticated interaction in 1-day, 7-day, and 30-day rolling windows.
- **Activation Rate**: Percentage of registered users completing email verification and profile bio/skills within 72 hours.
- **Job Discovery Conversion**: `(Applications Submitted / Unique Job Views) * 100`.
- **Hiring Pipeline Velocity**: Average days elapsed from `application.submitted` to `interview.completed`.
