# Kirmya Product Metrics & KPI Framework Manual

## 1. Platform Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **User Growth & Daily Activity**: Track Daily Active Users (DAU), Monthly Active Users (MAU), and DAU/MAU stickiness ratios.
- **Marketplace Liquidity**: Balance active job openings against qualified candidate applications across technical disciplines.
- **Learning & Skill Acquisition**: Measure assessment completion rates and skill endorsement growth across professional cohorts.
