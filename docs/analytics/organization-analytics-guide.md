# Kirmya Organization Hiring & Brand Analytics Guide

## 1. Enterprise Talent Acquisition & Employer Brand Metrics
- **Aggregate Hiring Velocity**: Measure organization-wide time-to-hire, offer acceptance ratios, and candidate drop-off stages.
- **Company Page Engagement**: Monitor employer branding views, open job impressions, and follower growth trends.
- **Cross-Organization Protection**: Multi-tenant authorization prevents any enterprise from accessing another organization's hiring metrics.
