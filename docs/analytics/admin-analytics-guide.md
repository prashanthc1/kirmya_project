# Kirmya Administrative Business Intelligence & Operations Analytics Guide

## 1. Executive Multi-Tab Control Center (`/admin/analytics`)
- **Platform Activation Funnel**: Monitor conversion rates across User Registration, Email Verification, Profile Completion, Job Search, and Applications.
- **Cohort Retention Grid**: Analyze weekly and monthly user cohorts to measure long-term platform engagement.
- **Trust & Safety Telemetry**: Track community incident reports, average resolution times, account restrictions, and appeal overturn rates.
