# Kirmya Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG 2.2 AA Compliance

## 1. Accessibility Standards & Verification Matrix
- **Keyboard Traps & Focus Management**: Modals, dropdowns, and drawers trap focus cleanly and support `Escape` dismissals.
- **Color Contrast Ratios**: 4.5:1 minimum contrast across all MUI v6 light and dark mode palette tokens.
- **Screen Reader Announcements**: Live region announcements (`aria-live="polite"`) for form errors and dynamic notifications.
